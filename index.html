How the guides work

Two levels

Yellow marks the five-minute guides, which have no equations. Black marks the in-depth guides, which include the equations and worked numbers. The buttons at the top of this page show one kind or the other.

Pick your beamline

The Settings menu at the top of How NE-CAT works, Why the X-ray beam is polarized and X-ray fluorescence at NE-CAT switches the figures and the worked numbers between 24-ID-C and 24-ID-E, before and after the APS Upgrade. Your choice follows you from one guide to the next.

Play with the figures

Most figures have sliders, and many of them move. Drag things around and see what changes. Nothing here can break.

Open the math if you want it

Every equation is folded away under its figure, with a plain reading of what it says and a worked number. One button opens them all.

Check the fine print

Values that are assumed or calculated, and not taken from a published source, are marked in the tables of How APS works and How NE-CAT works. Corrections are welcome.

NE-CAT, the Northeastern Collaborative Access Team, operates beamlines 24-ID-C and 24-ID-E for macromolecular crystallography at the Advanced Photon Source, Argonne National Laboratory. These guides were written for its users and staff. They work on a phone, but the figures are easier to explore on a larger screen.
